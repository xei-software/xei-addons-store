# XEI.ContoLavoro 1.2.1 – Release notes

## Features of this version

- The Subcontracting Job, the list of production orders, the mass transfer and the draft documents open maximized.

## Corrections in this version

- Finished product of the production order managed by batches: the receipt from production takes the batch from the incoming batch script.
- Finished product of the production order managed by serial numbers: the product receipt stops with a message that explains how to post it in SAP.
- Drafts of transfers and of scrap issues from warehouses with bin locations, with items managed by batches or serial numbers: the draft is created and the bin location is entered in SAP together with the batches.
