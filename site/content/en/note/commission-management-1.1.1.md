# XEI.CommissionManagement 1.1.1 – Release notes

## Corrections in this version

- The printed and emailed statement of commissions to settle and settled has no Net amount column, which applies to documents and not to individual installments: the net amount appears in the statement of calculated commissions.

## Installation

- Requires XEICore 1.2.1, included in the package.
- Installed with the SAP Business One Extension Manager, as an update of the installed version.
