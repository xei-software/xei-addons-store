# Privacy policy

Information pursuant to Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) for visitors to this
website and for those who request the free trial of a XEI application.

Effective from 27 October 2026.

## Data controller

**XEI S.r.l.**, Via Libertà 13, 27027 Gropello Cairoli (PV), Italy, VAT no. IT03055890184.
For any question about personal data: [info@xeisrl.com](mailto:info@xeisrl.com).

## Visiting the website

The website does not use cookies, does not collect browsing statistics and does not load content from
other websites. The language of the page is chosen from the browser's language settings, without
storing anything.

The website is hosted by GitHub Inc. (GitHub Pages), which records technical data about visits, such
as the IP address, for the security and operation of the service. GitHub Inc. is based in the United
States and participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the processing is described in GitHub's
privacy statement.

## Free trial request

The trial is requested with the form on the page of each application on the website or by email to
[license@xeisrl.com](mailto:license@xeisrl.com). The form data reach the XEI S.r.l. service that issues
the trial codes, hosted on Microsoft Azure in the European Union (Italy), which automatically sends the
trial code to the address given.

**Data processed**: requested application, email address, first and last name, company, VAT number,
country, language of the page and, if given, company code; in emails, also anything else written in
them.

**Purposes**: issuing the trial code, checking that the same company does not receive several trial
codes, answering questions about the application and the license, providing support during the trial.

To check for repeated requests, the service that issues the trial codes does not store the VAT number,
the email address and the domain in clear text, but only their fingerprint (SHA-256 hash), from which
the data cannot be derived, together with the trial code issued. The requests received are recorded in
a Microsoft SharePoint list of XEI S.r.l.

To limit abuse of the form, the service counts the requests of the day with a fingerprint (hash) of
the IP address they come from and of the email address given, never with the addresses in clear text.
These fingerprints are deleted every night.

**Legal basis**: pre-contractual measures taken at the request of the data subject (Article 6(1)(b)
GDPR); for the daily fingerprints of the IP address and the email, the legitimate interest of
XEI S.r.l. in the security of the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). The data are necessary to issue the trial code: without them, the trial cannot be activated.

**Retention**: 36 months from the request, if the trial is not followed by the purchase of a license.
For customers, for the duration of the relationship and then for the periods required by civil and tax
law.

## Who receives the data

The data are processed by authorized staff of XEI S.r.l. and by the providers of the email,
automatic code issuing and sending (Microsoft) and hosting (GitHub) services, appointed as data processors. They
are not sold or disclosed and are not used for advertising.

## Rights of the data subject

By writing to [info@xeisrl.com](mailto:info@xeisrl.com) you can request at any time access to your
data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and you can object to the
processing (Articles 15–22 GDPR).

You can always lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione
dei dati personali, [www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).
